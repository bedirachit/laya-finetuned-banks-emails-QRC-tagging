# Banking QRC label policy

Every email is from a retail-bank customer. Label the whole email with one class.

- **query** — the customer only wants information: a rate, a balance, a limit, branch hours, a code, or product terms. They are not asking the bank to change the account.
- **request** — the customer asks the bank to do something and is not expressing dissatisfaction. Examples: issue a cheque book, change a mobile number, raise a transfer limit, open a deposit, send a letter.
- **complaint** — the customer is unhappy about a charge, a failed or missing payment, a blocked card, or the service. If they are unhappy and also ask for a fix, the label is still **complaint**.

A duplicate fee plus a request to reverse it is a complaint. A calm instruction to issue a cheque book is a request. A question about the fixed-deposit rate is a query.
